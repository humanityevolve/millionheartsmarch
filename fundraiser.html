🌟 Your Impact

See how your donation helps build America's safest LGBTQ+ sanctuary

🏠

Safe Housing

Your donations help build emergency housing, transitional homes, and permanent residences for LGBTQ+ individuals who need a safe place to call home.

🏥

Healthcare Access

Fund on-site medical facilities, mental health services, and specialized LGBTQ+ healthcare that's often hard to find elsewhere.

🎓

Education & Skills

Support educational programs, job training, and skill development that help community members build independent, successful lives.

🤝

Community Building

Create spaces for community gatherings, support groups, cultural events, and the connections that make life meaningful.
