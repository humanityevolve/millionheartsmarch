📖 About Humanity Evolve

Creating Safe Spaces Where Love Wins

We're building America's safest LGBTQ+ community because everyone deserves to live authentically without fear.

🎯 Our Mission

💝 Why We Exist

LGBTQ+ individuals face disproportionate rates of violence, discrimination, and rejection. We're creating a sanctuary where love is celebrated, differences are honored, and safety is guaranteed.

  • ✓ 41% of transgender adults attempt suicide
  • ✓ 1 in 4 LGBTQ+ youth experience homelessness
  • ✓ 20% of hate crimes target LGBTQ+ individuals
  • ✓ Many face family rejection and workplace discrimination

🏳️‍🌈 Our Solution

A 160-acre secure community in Casa Grande, Arizona, where LGBTQ+ individuals and allies can live, work, and thrive in complete safety and acceptance.

  • ✓ 10-foot security walls around entire perimeter
  • ✓ 900+ container homes for residents
  • ✓ Community center with events and services
  • ✓ Memorial gardens honoring those we've lost

💔 Remembering Pulse

🕯️

June 12, 2016

49 beautiful souls were taken from us at Pulse Nightclub in Orlando. Their memory fuels our mission to create spaces where LGBTQ+ people can gather safely.

Our community will feature memorial gardens dedicated to the Pulse 49 and all LGBTQ+ lives lost to violence. We're planning our first major event for June 12, 2026 - the 10th anniversary - to honor their memory.

🏘️ Community Features

🏠

Container Homes

900+ modern, sustainable container homes for residents and visitors. Affordable housing in a safe environment.

🏢

Community Center

Events, meetings, support groups, and celebrations. The heart of our community where connections are made.

🌺

Memorial Gardens

Beautiful spaces honoring LGBTQ+ lives lost, including dedicated areas for the Pulse 49 and other victims of hate.

🛡️

Security Systems

10-foot walls, controlled access, 24/7 monitoring, and emergency response. Complete peace of mind.

🎉

Event Spaces

Pride celebrations, parties, weddings, and gatherings. Spaces designed for joy and celebration.

🌱

Sustainable Living

Solar power, water conservation, community gardens, and eco-friendly practices throughout.
